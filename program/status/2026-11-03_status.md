## What could move the dates

- **Rework rate.** About one package in two needs one round of rework today; the
  estimates include that.
- **Decision turnaround.** Every open question stops work until answered.
- **Migration scope.** How much the existing components must change to follow the
  contract is sized only when migration planning starts; this is the widest range.
- **CarMaker** depends on tool access; CARLA goes first without it.
