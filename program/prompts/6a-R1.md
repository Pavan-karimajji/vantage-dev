## Owner decisions to apply (do not reopen)
1. **Recorded controls (D17 (1)).** Replaying a recorded control, the orchestrator
   copies its `measurement_counter` and `source_timestamp_us`, writes its own
   `cycle_counter` and sticker OK, forces RESTART on the first run and after a jump,
   and otherwise passes the recorded `op_mode`. A recorded control is never handed
   over unchanged.
2. **Readiness limit (D17 (2)).** It is reported in
   `MeasDescription.readiness_limit_steps`. **0 = never waits** (a real limit: fall
   back at once). The run setup always has a limit (no default; missing is a load
   error) and the writer always writes it. "Positive integer" is wrong.
3. **Missing-sample signs (D17 (3)).** Counter gap, counter reset and stalled trigger
   are counted in the engine's event log; no badge kind until a reader needs one.
4. **refSync comparison (owner, 2026-10-30).** Accepted as written: a re-run's
   `refSync` is compared only on each entry's `measurement_counter` and
   `source_timestamp_us`; data outputs and `compState` are compared as *equal outputs*.
5. **Warming up (D17 (4)).** A stage started mid-recording, badged
   `KIND_WARMING_UP` until its outputs first equal the recorded outputs, and only
   when the recording holds them. "Every input matched by refSync" is what
   `KIND_FALLBACK` reports, not warming up.
6. **Seek.** `simEngineSeek` keeps the live measurement writer open: `MeasStatus.runId`
   does not change (one `runId` per writer start) and step numbers continue.
7. **Confirmed choices (D17 (5)).** A step at every instant where any event
   happens; no pause for now; the live measurement writer ships as a static
   library first.
